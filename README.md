# 🛍️ AI Shopping Growth Agent

An intelligent commerce analytics prototype that analyzes shopping data and converts it into actionable product growth recommendations.

## 🎯 Problem

E-commerce businesses collect large amounts of product data such as views, orders, ratings, and revenue. However, raw analytics do not directly tell merchants which products need attention or what action they should take.

## 💡 Solution

AI Shopping Growth Agent analyzes product performance and:

- Calculates conversion rates
- Identifies products needing attention
- Prioritizes growth opportunities
- Generates explainable recommendations
- Identifies the top growth opportunity
- Provides actionable business suggestions

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📊 Sales Overview
Displays:
- Total products
- Total views
- Total orders
- Total revenue

### 📈 Conversion Analysis
Calculates:

Conversion Rate = Orders / Views × 100

### ⚠️ Products Needing Attention
Automatically identifies products with low conversion rates.

### 🤖 Growth Recommendation Engine

The recommendation engine evaluates:

- Product views
- Conversion rate
- Customer rating

It assigns:
- Priority
- Action
- Reason
- Recommendation
- Expected impact

### 💡 Top Growth Opportunity

Automatically identifies the product with the lowest conversion rate and highlights it as a potential growth opportunity.
